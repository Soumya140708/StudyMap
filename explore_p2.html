Why StudyMap?

Your syllabus shouldn't feel scattered. StudyMap brings your subjects, topics and learning resources into one clear study path.

The problem students face

Studying becomes difficult when you don't know what to study, where to find it or what comes next.

01

Information is scattered

Notes, PDFs, textbooks, messages and online resources are often spread across different places.

02

You don't always know what's next

Different teaching orders can make it difficult to understand which topic you should prepare next.

03

Revision becomes harder

Before exams, students have to search through multiple sources to find the material they need.
