About StudyMap

StudyMap is a student-focused study platform designed to organize academic resources around the syllabus and help students prepare before a topic is taught in class.

🎓 What is StudyMap?

StudyMap provides students with a structured path through their academic syllabus. Instead of searching through different platforms for notes, reference material and practice questions, students can find resources connected to the topic they are studying.

The main idea is simple: Know what comes next, prepare before class, learn during class and revise afterwards.

❓ What Problem Does It Solve?

📚

Scattered Material

Study material can be spread across textbooks, Classroom, messages, PDFs and different websites.

🧭

Unclear Study Path

Students may not always know which topic they should study next according to their syllabus.

⏰

Last-Minute Preparation

Without an organized study path, students may end up preparing topics only before examinations.

✨ Benefits of StudyMap

For Students

Students can understand what they need to study next, access topic-based resources, revise important concepts and practice questions in one organized platform.

For Faculty

Faculty can maintain the syllabus structure and connect relevant learning resources without having to repeatedly create separate material for every stage of learning.

💡 What Makes StudyMap Different?

StudyMap is not simply a collection of notes. Its focus is on organizing learning around the syllabus.

Instead of students asking, "Where can I find the material?", StudyMap aims to answer, "What should I study next?"

The syllabus becomes the central roadmap, while notes, references, revision material and practice questions are connected to each topic.

🚀 Future Scope

StudyMap can be expanded beyond B.Sc. Computer Science to support other courses and departments. Future versions could include student accounts, dynamic progress tracking, faculty management, notifications and additional learning resources.
